# Campaigns

## What a Campaign Is

A campaign is the top-level configuration unit. It contains:

- domains
- Safe Page settings
- black settings
- scripts
- postbacks
- statistics

## Main Actions

From the dashboard you can:

- create a campaign
- duplicate a campaign
- open campaign statistics by clicking the campaign name
- open the editor through **Actions → Settings**
- delete a campaign

Renaming is available inside the campaign editor: click the pencil icon to the right of the campaign name at the top of the sidebar. A standard prompt asks for the new name.
